# Project Brief — Aster C1 Launch
_Owner: orchestrator · Last updated: 2026-11-15 · Track: Sprint (single page), scoped to direction + type + spacing only_

## Business
- What it is: Aster Cycles, a 14-person e-bike maker in Rotterdam.
- Offer / product: Aster C1, carbon commuter e-bike. EUR 6,200. 10.9 kg. Belt drive. Integrated lights. Launches March (year assumed: next March).
- Differentiator: a light, quiet, finished object. Craft and restraint rather than performance claims.

## Audience
- Primary: design-minded city commuters, 30-50, who read Monocle-type publications, would never buy "sporty" gear, and care about craft and calm.
- Secondary: design press, bike-shop partners (assumed).
- What they must believe after visiting: "This is a well-made object that makes my daily ride simpler, and the company behind it is serious."

## The feeling
Engineered and quiet, like a precision instrument. Not an extreme-sports ad.
