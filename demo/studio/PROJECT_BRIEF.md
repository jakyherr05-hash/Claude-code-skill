# Project Brief — Penumbra — Light Atelier (demo)
_Owner: orchestrator · Last updated: 2026-11-15_

> This is the studio's validation project. Penumbra is a **fictional** brand invented for the demo; no real company is depicted.

## Business
- What it is: an independent architectural-lighting design atelier (fictional), based in Lisbon, ~9 people.
- Offer: lighting concepts and detailed lighting design for sacred spaces, museums, hospitality and private houses; custom luminaires made with local craftspeople.
- Differentiator: they design with darkness and shadow first — light is placed only where it gives meaning. They specify colour temperature per hour of the day.

## Audience
- Primary: architects and interior architects looking for a lighting partner.
- Secondary: museum directors / private clients commissioning a building.
- What they must believe after visiting: "These people understand darkness as well as light — and they're precise."

## The feeling
Quiet awe, then trust in their precision.

## Goals & success measures
| Goal | Measure |
|---|---|
| Show the philosophy in under 10 seconds | Hero communicates "light shaped by darkness" without reading body copy |
| Prove craft & method | Visitors scroll through the method sequence |
| Start conversations | Contact CTA reachable from every section (nav) |
