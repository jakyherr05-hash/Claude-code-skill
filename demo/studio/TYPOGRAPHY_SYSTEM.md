## Pairing rationale
- **Contrast of structure:** a high-contrast serif (Newsreader) at light weight and very large size makes hairlines read as "a thin line of light on a dark wall"; a calm, neutral grotesk (Hanken) steps back for body and UI; mono (JetBrains) is the voice of precision, reserved for K, lux, degrees, years, index numbers and kickers.
- **opsz does the work of two fonts:** at 144px Newsreader sits at opsz 72 (fine hairlines, tight spacing); at 24-34px it adapts for pull-quotes automatically, so no second display face is needed.
- **Dark-ground legibility:** Newsreader 300 is used only from step-2 (24px) up. Everything below is Hanken 400+ so light-on-dark hairlines never get thin. Body on dark is 17-19px at 400 with +0.005em tracking and 1.6 line-height.
- Overused faces avoided (no Inter, Playfair, Instrument Serif, Cormorant, Space Grotesk).

## Rules
- Measure (characters per line): `--measure` = 64ch for body (CD limit ≤ 68ch; body sits on columns 2-7). Lead/pull-quote lines ≤ 28-34ch. Display may span columns 1-10 (CD) and uses `text-wrap: balance`.
- Optical adjustments: `font-optical-sizing: auto` on every Newsreader element (default in browsers, set explicitly on display). H1 gets `margin-left: -0.04em` to optically align the W's left edge with the body text edge. Hanging punctuation: use `text-indent: -0.4em` on quotes (`hanging-punctuation` is Safari-only). Kickers and mono lines are optically 1px lighter than they look; do not lower them below weight 420.
- Case, numerals (tabular/oldstyle), ligatures: kickers are uppercase mono at +0.12em. All numerals, units and index numbers set in `--font-mono` with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (also on the schedule table and the Hour readout so digits do not jitter while the slider moves). Prose numerals stay in Hanken's default lining figures. Newsreader's standard ligatures (fi, fl) stay on (visible in "first" in the specimen); disable with `font-variant-ligatures: none` only on mono. Body on dark is never below weight 400 and display weight 300 is never used below step-2 (24px).

## Fallbacks & metrics overrides (CLS)
Declared in `src/styles/fonts.css`. Calibrated in Chromium (Playwright) by measuring the summed advance width of real copy (H1, manifesto, a pangram) in the web font versus Liberation Serif/Sans/Mono, which are metric-identical to Times New Roman / Arial / Courier New; the `local()` names therefore behave the same on Windows/macOS. Georgia was not used because it is not installed on the test machine and could not be measured.

| Fallback | Base | size-adjust | ascent / descent / line-gap | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Newsreader Fallback | Times New Roman | 105% | 70% / 25.2% / 0% | width ratio 1.059 at 100px, 0.99 at 18px, 0.978 at 24px; tuned for display (H1 is the LCP). hhea 1470/-530 of 2000 upm ÷ 1.05 |
| Hanken Fallback | Arial | 100.8% | 99.2% / 30.1% / 0% | width ratio 1.0076; hhea 1000/-303 of 1000 ÷ 1.008 |
| JetBrains Mono Fallback | Courier New | 100% | 102% / 30% / 0% | both have 600/1000 advance; hhea 1020/-300 |

Stacks: `--font-display: 'Newsreader Variable', 'Newsreader Fallback', Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif` and equivalents for text and mono. Residual risk: below ~30px Newsreader is up to ~2% narrower than the fallback (opsz-dependent), so a small reflow is possible on pull-quotes; the H1 matches within the measurement error. Not yet measured with real swap CLS in Lighthouse (hand to perf-engineer).
