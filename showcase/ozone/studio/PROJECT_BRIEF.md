# Project Brief — OZONE — Clear Air (youth vaping prevention)
_Owner: orchestrator · Last updated: 2026-11-17_

## Business
- What it is: OZONE, a smoke shop (adult retailer of tobacco/vape products).
- Offer / product: this page sells nothing. It is OZONE's public stance on youth vaping: a scroll-driven story about how vaping harms kids and teens, and what OZONE does about it.
- Differentiator: a smoke shop saying the honest thing out loud. Credibility comes from accuracy and restraint, not from scare tactics.

## Audience
- Primary: parents, guardians, teachers and local community members.
- Secondary: teens who land on the page (it must speak to them without preaching or making vaping look appealing), adult customers.
- What they must believe after visiting: nicotine is especially harmful to a developing brain; youth products are designed to look harmless; OZONE does not sell to anyone under 21 and backs that with action; there is free help to quit.

## The feeling
One sentence: the moment a sweet-smelling cloud clears and you see what was inside it.

## Goals & success measures
| Goal | Measure |
|---|---|
| Story understood without reading every word | each scroll beat carries one idea in ≤ 12 words of display copy (ANIMATION_SPECIFICATION story-beat table) |
| Facts are trustworthy | every number on the page has a cited primary source (CDC, FDA, U.S. Surgeon General, NIH/NIDA, Truth Initiative) listed in a Sources section |
| Visitors leave with an action | parents: conversation guide + free quit line; teens: free, confidential quit help; community: report a sale to a minor |
| Premium, award-level craft | Gate B creative PASS with the visual-critique rubric at 390/768/1024/1440 |
| Fast and accessible | WCAG 2.2 AA (axe 0 serious/critical), reduced-motion path complete, mobile LCP ≤ 2.5 s, CLS ≤ 0.05 |

## Constraints
- Stack / hosting: static single page (no other routes), deployable to any static host.
- Deadline: none given; quality first.
- Accessibility / legal: WCAG 2.2 AA. Must not promote or glamorise vaping, show products, flavours or prices, or appeal to minors. Health claims only from primary sources, quoted carefully. No fear-mongering imagery of sick children.
- Budget for paid fonts/assets/services: none — free/open licences only.

## Likes / dislikes (user-supplied references)
- User: "I don't want something basic at all, I want something amazing." Storytelling scroll animation. Landing page only — no extra pages.

## Assumptions (made by the studio, revisit if wrong)
- OZONE is a real retailer; the page is its own youth-prevention statement, so the tone is a responsible business speaking, not a government campaign.
- U.S. context (federal minimum age 21, U.S. data and help lines). Revisit if OZONE is outside the U.S.
- No logo supplied: a typographic wordmark is used and flagged as replaceable.
- Address/phone/hours are placeholders marked `[ ]` for the client to fill in.
