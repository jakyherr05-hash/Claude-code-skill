# Creative Direction — OZONE — Clear Air (youth vaping prevention)
_Owner: creative-director · Status: draft (for approval) · Date: 2026-11-17_

## Concept (one sentence)
**The Layer:** OZONE is one thin layer between the cloud and kids, so the page lets a sweet haze in, shows plainly what it carries, then draws a line the haze cannot cross.

Why it can't belong to anyone else: only a retailer has a counter to hold a line at. A government campaign can't say "not at our counter". The name supports it without being spelled out: ozone is the thin layer that keeps harm out of the air. The page never explains this. The picture does: a thin layer of blue light at a horizon, held haze above it and clear air below. The tensions to hold are honest ↔ warm, atmospheric ↔ exact, a seller ↔ a neighbour, and calm ↔ urgent.

## Narrative arc
The pacing runs establish (1), tension (2–4), reveal (5), weight (6–7), turn (8), release (9–10), then Sources. Only beats 5 and 8 pin, for 3.8 viewports in total. Everything else scrolls freely over a fixed fog stage, as sticky-stage steps with captions. The display copy below is a final draft in US English. Before the turn, one word per statement is *clear* (full ink, shown in **bold**) and the rest is *hazed*. From the turn on, every word is clear: the page clears.

## Design language & rationale
- **Primary language: cinematic storytelling.** The page is a film the visitor directs by scrolling: one atmospheric medium, two pinned scenes, and words in sync with the picture. This answers "amazing, not basic".
- **Accent language: editorial, in its public-interest data-journalism form.** That means date-stamped citation tags, plain hairline charts and an open Sources chapter.
- A smoke shop only earns credibility with parents through accuracy. So the page borrows the manners of reporting and the medium of film.
- Rejected registers:
  - Campaign, brutalist or meme styling. From a retailer it reads as pandering, and it is the register teens find appealing.
  - Luxury. It would glamorise the shop.
  - Corporate. It is forgettable.
- **Edge language:** hairline, 0 radius, no shadows. The only glow on the page is the ozone line.

## Type personality (final fonts in TYPOGRAPHY_SYSTEM.md)
- **Personality:** a public notice set by a careful neighbour. Upright, plain-spoken, very large, never shouting. Display is sentence case, 400–500 weight, tight negative tracking: one very large statement per viewport against small, precise mono labels. Never all caps or condensed-bold campaign faces (the Anton/Bebas register).
- **Families:** two. A grotesk for display and text, and a mono for citation tags, the chapter counter (`05 / 10`), chart labels and the inventory list.
- **Free/OFL direction (typography-spacing to verify):** Instrument Sans, whose `wdth` axis lets 390 tighten without shrinking, or Schibsted Grotesk; with IBM Plex Mono or JetBrains Mono.
- **Language:** US English throughout (flavored, vapor).

## Image & video direction
- **Medium:** one procedural full-screen fragment shader of slow, low-octave haze, fixed behind the story. No photos, video, 3D models, illustrations or icons. No products, devices, flavors, people, hands or mouths.
- **Look: stale indoor haze, not a plume.** Flat and low contrast, drifting slowly sideways in neutral grey on graphite. No light sources, rim or back light, colour, billows, curls, rings, exhale shapes or bright white volumes: nothing about the cloud may look appealing. Monochrome grain at 3 %, over the haze only.
- **Shader states:** soft (beats 1–4) → focused particulate (5) → banded high (6–7) → held above the line (8 on) → none in clear air. Driven by the uniforms `density`, `focus`, `bandY`, `lineY` and `textMask`. Half-resolution buffer: DPR ≤ 1 on mobile, ≤ 1.5 on desktop.
- **Fallbacks:** a static poster per state (AVIF ≤ 40 KB), serving first paint, no-WebGL and reduced motion.
- **Legibility plan:** the shader masks density down to `fog-cap` inside each breakpoint's text-safe zone. Text ≤ 18 px never sits on fog; it goes on graphite or `graphite-raised` panels. The `hazed` tone is used only at display sizes. QA measures the worst pixel under the glyphs at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440.
- **Diagrams:** 1 px hairlines in ink-muted with mono labels. Each fact gets one simple form, and no number ever stands alone without one.

## Motion personality
- **Tempo:** weather, not fireworks. Slow, calm and exact.
- **Principles:** purposeful; responsive to the user (the story is scrubbed, scrub ≈ 0.8, never timed); quiet by default.
- **Ambient drift:** about one screen-width per 40 s. It pauses off-screen, in a hidden tab, and under reduced motion.
- **Reveals:** line-masked, 0.8–1.0 s, fired once.
- **Hover:** a hairline underline draws in 0.2 s.
- **Signature ease `clear`:** slow start, long settle (gsap-motion defines the curve). It is used for the recurring micro-device, where a statement's key word goes from `hazed` to `ink` as it reaches mid-viewport, and for the turn. Text is never animated from invisible.
- **What never moves:** body copy, numbers (no count-ups), citations, buttons, the header and Sources. No parallax on text. Scroll must feel native, and nothing changes its speed.
- **Opening (readable by about 1.5 s, no gate, no preloader, no audio):** at 0 ms the HTML paints the graphite ground and poster haze, with the H1 (the LCP element) and skip link visible. At about 300 ms "kids" clears; at about 800 ms the ozone hairline draws along the bottom edge. After idle, WebGL crossfades in over a matching poster across 1.2 s, so the first frame doesn't jump.
- **Header:** small wordmark (placeholder, replaceable), mono chapter counter, and "Get help" linking to beat 10. The skip link is first in tab order. The canvas is `aria-hidden`.

## Signature moment(s)
1. **Look closer (beat 5).** For 180vh, the haze closes in (0–40 %). Then `focus` resolves softness into specks while the callout leaders draw in reading order (40–75 %), followed by a rest (25 %). This is the brief's "see what was inside it", made calm and scientific rather than scary. It uses the same shader, so it costs no new assets. Reduced motion shows the focused poster with callouts.
2. **The layer (beat 8).** For 200vh: the first 20 % is silence. Then the ozone line rises (20–70 %). Below it, density goes to 0 behind a 2 px soft edge and the ground turns to `air`; above it, the haze is held, with an `ozone` glow along the upper side. The pledge then lands, followed by a rest. When the pin releases, the line becomes the top rule of beat 9, and the haze scrolls away above and never returns. Reduced motion shows the static split composition.

## Closing actions (beats 8–10 and Sources)
- **OZONE pledge.** "No one under 21. We check ID, every time." It is phrased only as OZONE's policy, with federal law cited as F11. Never compare OZONE with other shops. Any further commitment appears only if the client confirms it, otherwise it stays a `[ ]` placeholder.
- **Parents and community (beat 9).** Call 1-800-QUIT-NOW (free help, any age, F12) via `tel:`. "Talk with your teen" links to an official conversation guide, and "Report a sale to a minor" links to the FDA reporting route: both are **SOURCE PENDING** and appear only once an F-row exists.
- **Teens (beat 10).** `sms:47848` with body QUIT (F13, ages 13–17); teen.smokefree.gov (F12); smokefree.gov "Are you addicted to vaping?" (F14). Do not write "confidential" or "anonymous" until verified.
- **Sources.** An open, numbered list with no accordion; each row gives publisher, page title, date and URL. Every on-page citation tag (mono, for example "FDA · NYTS 2025") links to its row. The list ends with "Facts checked 2026-11-17". F8 is unused (one idea per beat) and may be listed.

## Deliberately not doing
- **Scare imagery.** No lungs, brains, sick or coughing kids, hospitals, skulls or X-rays. No shock-stat flashes, and no number counting up.
- **People, products and flavors.** No people at all. No devices, pods, packaging, prices or flavor names used as colour or lure.
- **Glamour clouds.** No coloured or backlit vapor, plumes, rings, neon, or soft white "dreamy" volumes. No pastel or candy palette.
- **Preaching or shaming.** Nothing like "Don't do it" aimed at teens. No peer slang, memes, stickers, emoji or Gen-Z voice. No all-caps campaign type.
- **Hidden or gated content.** No carousels, sliders, tabs or accordions hiding facts. No gate, preloader, click-to-start or audio. No cursor followers, hijacked scroll speed, floating CTA blobs or pop-ups.
- **Unsupported claims.** No claims about other retailers. No "safer than smoking", no pod-vs-pack, no unsourced numbers.
- **Wording to avoid.** Never use the phrase "thin blue line". Never explain the brand name as a pun.

## Alternates considered
- **The Label.** The page as a giant, honest ingredient and warning label, one fact per line item. It is truthful and cheap, but it is static and cold, and labels are packaging language that sits too close to the product. Rejected.
- **Two Clocks.** A brain-development clock and an addiction clock running side by side down the scroll. It is precise, but a countdown pushes it into the scare register. Rejected; its age line survives in beat 6.
- **The Window.** Scrolling wipes a fogged window clear. It implies watching a child through glass, which is a surveillance tone, and it fights text legibility. Rejected.
