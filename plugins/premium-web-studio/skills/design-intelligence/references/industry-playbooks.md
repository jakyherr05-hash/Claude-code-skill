# Industry playbooks

What each audience expects (break carefully), what usually makes the difference, and the trap to avoid.

| Industry | Expectations to honour | Where to break convention | Must-have content | Typical signature moment | Trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Luxury brand | Calm, discretion, craft, heritage | Unexpected material close-ups; radical negative space | Story of making, collection, boutique/contact | Slow mask reveal of a single hero object | Slowness that delays content |
| Automotive | Performance, design, specs, configurator | Editorial spec sheets; sound-free cinematic scroll | Models, specs, pricing/build, test drive | Pinned scroll camera orbit / image sequence | Huge sequences on mobile |
| Technology / SaaS | Clarity, proof, product UI, pricing | Product demo as narrative timeline | Value prop, product, social proof, pricing, docs link | Scroll-scrubbed product walkthrough | Fake UI, buzzword soup |
| E-commerce | Fast browsing, trust, easy checkout | Editorial brand bands between product grids | Products, price, shipping/returns, reviews | Flip-animated filtering; add-to-cart micro-moment | Motion in the purchase path |
| Fashion | Seasonal attitude, campaign imagery | Typographic collisions, horizontal lookbooks | Collection, campaign, stores/shop | Image-to-detail page transition | Products impossible to find |
| Creative agency | Personality, work first, culture | Interaction as manifesto | Work, about, contact, careers | Cursor-follow project previews; WebGL hero | Gimmick over portfolio |
| Product launch | One object, desire, date, CTA | Countdown → reveal choreography | Product, key features, availability, CTA | Scroll-exploded 3D / sticky product with captions | Missing CTA/price |
| Architecture portfolio | Rigour, light, space, materials | Plans and sections as typographic art | Projects, process, studio, contact | Clip-path reveals mimicking light across a plan | Tiny images, slow galleries |
| Corporate | Trust, clarity, findability | Characterful display type; real photography | Services, proof, people, contact, news | Data story with animated numbers | Carousels and jargon |
| Interactive storytelling | Immersion, pacing, emotion | The whole page is the medium | Chapters, skip/nav, credits | Pinned chapters synced with picture | Scroll-jacking without control |

## Audience calibration

- **Older or enterprise audiences:** larger body (18–20px), stronger contrast, conventional nav, motion that confirms rather than performs.
- **Design-literate audiences:** can handle unconventional nav and denser type play — still keep a visible way home.
- **Mobile-majority audiences:** design 390px first; replace hover/cursor effects with tap and scroll equivalents; keep LCP media small.
- **International audiences:** choose fonts with the required scripts; allow 30–40% text expansion for translations.
